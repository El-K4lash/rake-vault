## Description (à coller dans Creator Hub > Configure > Description) — v2 du 23/10, 937 caractères sur 1000
❄️ Grab your shovel, dig through the snow and find RARE EGGS hidden under it! 🥚✨

⛏️ DIG – Shovel snow all over a giant winter mountain and fill your sled
💰 SELL – Full sled? Sell it and buy bigger shovels & sleds
🥚 HATCH – Eggs hatch into pets inside your cozy chalet
💸 EARN – Your pets make money every second, even while you dig!

🏔️ REBIRTH to climb 7 worlds: Snowy Village → Pine Forest → Frozen Lake → Ice Cave → Glacier Summit → Aurora Peak → 🎅 North Pole
🐧 87 pets to collect, with 6 mutations: Gold, Diamond, Lava, Galaxy, Rainbow & Hacker
🌨️ Snowstorms refill every zone… and GOLDEN STORMS drop golden eggs with x3 luck!
⚡ Dig combos, rare Frozen Chests and 12 shovels to unlock

🎄 Advent Calendar: a free gift every day until Christmas
🎟️ Winter Pass (30 tiers) · 📜 Daily quests · 🏆 Weekly leaderboards

🎁 CODES: SNOW · SHOVEL · LUCKY · XMAS · LAUNCH (launch week only!)

👍 Like & ⭐ Favorite so you don't miss the next update!
